# Book chapter coverage

This reference maps all 38 CKA chapters and 31 CKS chapters to their actual published task steps and verification scripts. The machine-readable source is [coverage.json](coverage.json); the author maps are preserved in [source-maps](source-maps/).

A mapping means the exercise is implemented. Hosted execution results are recorded separately in the validation reports and evidence directory. The bootstrap exercise uses two supported VMs, and the HA exercise uses three real control-plane containers on one VM; each task explains its topology adaptation.
